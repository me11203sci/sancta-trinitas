Learned men of the world, educate me, what [[Principle|principle]] perpetuates your [[existence]]?  Put aside the difficult question of our origins, or that immaterial notion of our [[End|end]], and elucidate with me how we are now.

## RATIO VT IMAGO DEI
Scrutinize yourself for the genesis of our inquiry, and in doing so realize the most obvious subject of contemplation: yourself. What are you? [[Human|Man]]? Well, [[Anthropology|what is man]]?
