One of the [[Three Laws of Thought|three laws of thought]], which simply put, states that a [[proposition]] and its [[negation]] cannot be held to be [[Truth|true]] simultaneously and in the same sense.
- Formally, for proposition $P$, $\lnot (P\land\lnot P)$. Note that this expression is a [[Tautology|tautology]].
